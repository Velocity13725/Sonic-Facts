A legacy at full throttle

History in a flash.

From a 16-bit icon to a global pop-culture hero, the blue blur has kept finding new ways to run.

1991

The first spin dash

Sonic the Hedgehog debuts on the Sega Genesis / Mega Drive, bringing speed-focused platforming to the spotlight.

1992–1994

The classic era

Tails joins the adventure, Knuckles enters the rivalry, and the classic games expand Sonic's world.

1998

A new dimension

Sonic Adventure takes the series into a major 3D adventure on the Dreamcast.

2001

Shadow arrives

Sonic Adventure 2 introduces Shadow, a rival whose story becomes a fan favourite.

Today

The adventure continues

New games, remasters, comics, animation, and films keep bringing Sonic to new audiences.
